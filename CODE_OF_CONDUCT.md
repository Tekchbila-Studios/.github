# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, never about the person
writing it.

## What we expect

- **Criticise the work, not the author.** "This drops the error" is useful.
  "You clearly didn't read the docs" is not, and it makes the first point harder
  to hear.
- **Assume competence and good intent.** People arrive with different context,
  not less intelligence. A question that seems obvious to you was not obvious to
  them, and answering it well is cheaper than the alternative.
- **Accept that "no" is a complete answer.** Maintainers decline changes for
  reasons they may not have time to litigate in full.
- **Respect people's stated identity**, including names and pronouns.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment in any form; sexual attention or advances; insults, slurs, or
demeaning comments about anyone's identity — including race, gender, gender
identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size,
age, nationality, or religion; publishing others' private information without
permission; sustained disruption of discussion; and encouraging any of the
above.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code and comments,
and any other space where you are participating in this organization's projects.

## Reporting

Email <contact@infragr.am> with "conduct" in the subject line. Reports go to the
studio owner and are kept confidential. Include what happened, where, and links
if you have them.

You will get a response within 5 business days. If your report concerns the
person you would be reporting to, say so — we will bring in an uninvolved third
party rather than have them handle it.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern, the response is one of: a private word, a
public correction, a temporary ban from the organization's spaces, or a
permanent one. We will tell you which and why.

Reporting in good faith never counts against you, even if we conclude no action
is warranted.
